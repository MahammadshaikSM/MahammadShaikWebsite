# Shaik Mahammad (Moh) · Workday Integration & Reporting Consultant

Personal portfolio for 6+ years of enterprise Workday integration work across Deloitte, IBM and Accenture.

**Live site:** https://mahammadshaiksm.github.io/MahammadShaikWebsite/

## Themes

The site ships one engine with four themes. Switch between them with the colored dots in the nav.

| Theme | Path | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Arizona (home) | `/` | ASU maroon & gold; a low-poly Sonoran desert where the sun sets as you scroll |
| Ember | `/ember/` | Black and orange; a 3D card that flips on scroll |
| Liquid | `/liquid/` | Light theme; a field of dots that ripples like water |
| Neon | `/neon/` | Black with a neon accent; a glossy egg still life |

## What's inside

- Hero with a short claim, one call to action, proof chips and pausable achievement notifications
- Impact counters with numbered footnotes explaining how figures were measured
- Experience at Deloitte, IBM and Accenture, with expandable highlights
- "Secure by design" section covering Workday integration security
- Bento grid of skills, certifications and education
- Mesa Signal preview: a demo of a crypto & stock prediction venture, with illustrative (not live) prices, a pause control, color-blind-safe gain/loss colors and an AI card that waits for approval
- Interactive particle footer

Accessibility: every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, focus states are visible, gains and losses use sign and arrow as well as color, and layouts are tested at phone width.

## Editing content

All text lives in `assets/content.js`: experience, skills, certifications, education, contact links and the venture name. Edit it once and every theme updates.

## Built with

Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 and [GSAP](https://gsap.com/) 3.12 with ScrollTrigger from cdnjs. There's no build step: GitHub Pages serves the files as they are.
